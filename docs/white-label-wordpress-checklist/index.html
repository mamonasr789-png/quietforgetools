FAQ

Is this a static / local-only tool?

Yes. The deliverable is static HTML, CSS, and JS. Visitors mark checks in the browser. It does not crawl or automatically scan websites.

Who hosts it?

You do. Upload the folder anywhere you host static files (your site, CDN, GitHub Pages, etc.). Hosting and domain setup are not included.

Does QuietForgeTools receive visitor data?

No. The checklist does not send visitor marks or reports to QuietForgeTools. Nothing is uploaded by the checklist itself.

What happens after I request this setup?

Email with the intake fields. We confirm the fixed £49 scope, then arrange payment after confirmation. Once all required inputs are received, we deliver the static package within 2 business days and note the revision window.

What counts as one revision?

One round of reasonable adjustments to logo, colours, business name, and CTA wording or URL within 7 days of delivery. New checklist items, redesigns, hosting work, and WordPress integration are out of scope.
